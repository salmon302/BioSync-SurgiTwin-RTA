# SRS v1.1 Change Proposal (SurgiTwin-RTA)

Proposal only. SRS.md remains v1.0.0 until the document owner approves these amendments.

Target document: SRS.md v1.0.0, 100 lines. Every line number below refers to that version.
Findings: SNDEV/docs/impl-2026-10-13-srs-baseline-validation.md, Blockers VAL-001 through VAL-004.
Replacement text sources: vv/traceability/vtm.md (31 rows, interim authoritative) and vv/tests/protocol-register.md (67 rows at the time of writing).
Prepared: 2026-10-14.

Conventions used in the tables:

* Current text strings are quoted exactly from SRS.md v1.0.0 and shortened to the relevant clause.
* Acceptance-metric replacement text is the Acceptance Metric cell of the cited row in vv/traceability/vtm.md with the "(proposed; supersedes SRS wording per VAL-0NN)" marker removed.
* SRS Section 5 holds six columns: Requirement ID, Subsystem / Module, Regulatory / Standard Mapping, CSA Risk Classification, Verification Method & Toolchain, Target Acceptance Metric. Proposed extract rows keep those six columns, shorten the method cell to the named technique and toolchain, and drop the VTM Test Method column, which stays in vv/traceability/vtm.md.
